Explore The Process

Land Acquisition

CleanHedge identifies and secures suitable land for the solar plant installation in one of their solar parks.

Transmission Line Setup

Infrastructure is developed to connect the solar plant to the grid,ensuring the smooth transfer of energy.

Energy Consumption Assessment

CleanHedge evaluates the client's past and current power usage to design an optimized solar solution tailored to their energy needs.

Tailored Solar + BESS Design

A customized solar and battery storage (BESS) system is engineered based on the client’s energy profile, site conditions, and future goals.

Project Execution

CleanHedge manages the end-to-end installation, commissioning, and quality checks to ensure seamless execution of the solar project.

System Activation & Grid injection

The solar energy generated is fed into the grid, which is then set off against the clients electricity bill.

O&M

Operations and maintenance are undertaken by CleanHedge to ensure optimal system performance, uptime, and long-term reliability.
