Solar Rooftop

Solar Rooftop Projects allow consumers to generate their own electricity by installing solar panels on the rooftop of their offices, factories, or commercial buildings. These systems are typically connected to the local electricity grid and can be used to power a building's energy needs while exporting excess power back to the grid (net metering).


Lowering electricity bills is one of the main reasons why consumers may decide to install rooftop solar panels. Every company is different-from the size of the rooftop to the electricity requirement.


CleanHedge will inspect your rooftop as well as your electricity bill to give you the right advice whether to opt for rooftop or open access solar or both.

Types of Solar Open Access Models

Grid-Tied Solar
Rooftop System
(With Net Metering):
  • Connected directly to the grid.
  • Uses solar power during the day, draws from the grid when needed.
  • Net metering allows the export of excess electricity and gets adjusted in monthly bills.
  • Best for: Homes, offices, schools, hospitals, and factories with regular daytime consumption and enough rooftop space to meet 50% or more of the power requirement

Hybrid System
(Grid + Battery):
  • Combines solar panels, batteries, and grid connectivity.
  • Stores excess solar energy for use during power cuts or nighttime.
  • Provides backup energy and reduces reliance on diesel generators
  • Best for: Areas with unreliable grid power, businesses needing an uninterrupted supply or consumers experiences very high peak rates.

Off-Grid System:

  • Fully independent from the grid.
  • Utilization of total generation in the day or stores all generated power in batteries.
  • Suitable for plants with large rooftop space or remote locations in areas with no grid access.
  • Best for: Remote homes, farms, or telecom towers in off-grid areas.
