Solar InRoof

India’s first integrated solar roofing solution

The Ornate InRoof provides a unique solar roof solution. Solar panels are transformed into the primary roof of the building, eliminating the need for metal/sheet roofing. This leak-proof solar solution generates more energy than traditional solar installations and can be customized as per your unique energy needs!


InRoof not only makes your space energy-efficient but also aesthetically pleasing. With over 20 MW installations done in 2 years across India, it is the roof of the future!


Because Solar was never meant to look bad. It just requires a conscious effort to make it look good!

Benefits of Solar InRoof

Power Advantage

Generates 10% More Energy Due to Free Air-flow Under Panels and better heat efficiency

Space Optimized

Accommodates 26% More Panels in the Same Space

Clean Aesthetics

An Aesthetic Installation Without Protruding Hardware

Expansion Proof

Absorbs Expansion & Contraction Factors, Avoiding Cell Cracks

Shield Layer

Leak Proof System With Climate Resistant Seal

Custom Fit

    Can be Adapted to all roof styles and applications
