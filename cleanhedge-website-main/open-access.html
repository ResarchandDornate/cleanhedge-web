Open Access

The Open Access mechanism allows consumers to generate electricity from renewable power generators, bypassing traditional utility companies. At CleanHedge Verde Pvt Ltd we enable Commercial and Industrial consumers to source solar power remotely, from our solar parks.


Through government-supported regulations, businesses with a contracted demand of 1 MW or more (in most states) can generate or purchase solar power through the grid under different Open Access models. In Maharashtra, A green energy open access (GEOA) policy approved by the regulatory authority allows consumers with smaller loads to avail Open Access.


At CleanHedge Verde, we can meet all your business’ electricity needs by developing solar projects for our customers across Maharashtra at our off-site private solar farms (in Wardha and Solapur). Moreover, this model does away with the limitation of onsite solar (which is dependent on the area, shadow, space limitation etc ) as it is a scalable option which is not constrained by availability of space at your facility.


The cost of power through Solar Open access is lower than grid tariffs, thus providing a cost saving solution through green technology. Further, the capacity availed through open access is much higher than rooftop solar allowing for more units to be offset. This model is especially attractive for Commercial and Industrial (C&I) consumers aiming to meet sustainability goals while also saving on power costs.

Types of Solar Open Access Models

100% Captive Open Access:
  • Ownership-Based: The consumer owns 100% equity in the solar power project.
  • Charges: Wheeling and transmission charges applicable as well as AMC charges
  • Benefits: Exemptions from CSS and additional surcharges in most states. High power cost saving.
  • Investment IRR: With Debt, returns are comparable to Group captive model
  • ROI: 4-5yrs
  • Tax Benefits: Can be claimed with this model.

100% Captive Open Access:
  • Ownership-Based: The consumer owns 100% equity in the solar power project.
  • Charges: Wheeling and transmission charges applicable as well as AMC charges
  • Benefits: Exemptions from CSS and additional surcharges in most states. High power cost saving.
  • Investment IRR: With Debt, returns are comparable to Group captive model.
  • ROI: 4-5yrs

Group Captive Open Access:
  • Ownership-Based: The consumer owns 26% equity in the solar power project.
  • Charges: Wheeling and transmission charges applicable as well as AMC charges.
  • Benefits: Exemptions from CSS and additional surcharges in most states. Limited power cost saving
  • Investment IRR: High recovery of upfront capital.
  • ROI: 2-3 yrs

Third-Party
PPA Open Access:

  • Ownership-Based:No ownership, third-party owns the solar power project.
  • Charges: Wheeling and transmission charges applicable as well as AMC charges.
  • Benefits: Marginal Saving of Power cost with zero investment. Short term contracts available.
  • ROI: Not applicable.
  • Tax Benefits:Not applicable
