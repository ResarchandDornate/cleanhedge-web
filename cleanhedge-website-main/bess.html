Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)

Installing a BESS can help expand your business by becoming energy independent. To increase grid stability using renewable power, we use a battery storage system. It consists of solar panels which inject power and store the same in a battery during high solar radiation times. This energy is released into the system during times of low or no sunlight.

CleanHedge Energy Storage System (ESS)

CleanHedge ESS is a next-generation Battery Energy Storage Solution(BESS)designed to maximize energy efficiency, safety, and scalability for modern power systems. Built using advanced Lithium Iron Phosphate(LFP) technology and backed by precision-engineered components, CleanHedge ESS delivers high energy density,real-time control, and unmatched reliability.


From industrial-scale renewable integration to grid support and backup power,CleanHedge ESS is a plug-and-playsolution ready to power the future.
